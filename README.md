# AI-Resume-Screening-Job-Matching-System
AI-powered system to automate resume screening and intelligently match candidates with job roles using semantic understanding.

Problem Statement

There has been inefficiency in the hiring process that arises because of the numerous numbers of applications that come up during the recruitment period. The number of resumes that have to be reviewed manually may range in several hundreds or thousands, and as a result, this may make it prone to human errors or biases, whereby there may be some very competent candidates who end up being overlooked.
This problem can be identified among the recruiter, hiring manager, and job applicant. The recruiter finds it challenging to cope with the workload while the job applicant may not find a job regardless of their competencies.

Why This Problem Is Important
Inefficient recruitment hinders an organization from achieving its productivity goals.

Understanding of the Problem 

Major problems:
Resume review is time-consuming
There is a possibility of bias and inconsistencies in recruitment decisions

Why this problem exists:
High number of applicants for every position
No smart systems that can comprehend resumes intelligently

My assumptions:
There is a need for a fast and efficient method of reviewing resumes
Job seekers want an unbiased approach to evaluating their qualifications

Research on Existing  Solutions

Existing solutions or tools:
LinkedIn Job Matching: Offers job suggestions depending on the information provided in the profile but is highly dependent on keywords and user inputs
Applicant Tracking System (ATS): Employed by organizations for sorting resumes based on keyword searches and predefined algorithms

Observations:
Current solutions concentrate on keyword matching rather than semantic comprehension
Manual intervention is required and yet fails to identify qualified candidates

Limitations identified:
No comprehension of semantics in the resume/job description
Cannot prioritize candidates according to their appropriateness

My Identified Gap (Novelty)

The current systems do not have the capability to properly comprehend the context within which the skills and experience of the candidates exist. The current approach of using the keywords leads to wrong screening out of qualified candidates.

There is a clear gap in systems that can:
Interpret the resume in a manner similar to a human
Properly compare the candidates’ resumes to the required job positions
Produce appropriate ranking by considering relevancy rather than just the keywords

My Idea:
My idea is the creation of a smart system that will have the ability to understand the resume with the help of language interpretation in order to provide better matching to the job position requirements.

My Proposed Approach
Proposed approach to solving the problem:
The proposed system will automatically analyze resumes and job postings, extract relevant information, and match candidates on the basis of their relevance to a particular job posting.

Possible features:
Automatic parsing and extraction of resume information
Matching of resumes to job postings
Ranking of resumes according to their relevancy
Results dashboard to help recruiters see and control the results
Challenges to Consider

What challenges may come up as part of your approach?
How to ensure that the model understands different resume formats?
How to avoid biases during model training and evaluation?
What can be done about incomplete or inconsistent information within resumes?

What I Learned
Recruitment is not only a technical issue; it’s also an ethical and judgment-based one.
Current systems have limitations that do not completely address the issue.
AI can be highly effective for optimizing the recruiting process.
It’s essential to understand the problem first before implementing a solution.
