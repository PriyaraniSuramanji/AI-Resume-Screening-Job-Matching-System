## Abstract
The AI Resume Screening & Job Matching System is designed to automate the recruitment process by intelligently analyzing resumes and matching them with job descriptions. The system uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning techniques to extract candidate information such as skills, education, experience, and certifications from resumes. It then compares the extracted information with job requirements and generates a matching score. The system helps recruiters quickly identify suitable candidates, reduces manual screening efforts, improves hiring efficiency, and minimizes bias in recruitment decisions.
Keywords— Artificial Intelligence (AI), Resume Screening, Job Matching, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Resume Parsing, Machine Learning, Candidate Ranking, TF-IDF, BERT, Recruitment Automation.
________________________________________

# 1. Introduction
Recruitment is one of the most important processes in any organization. With the increasing number of job applications, recruiters spend a significant amount of time reviewing resumes manually. Traditional screening methods are often slow, inefficient, and prone to human bias.

The AI Resume Screening & Job Matching System addresses these challenges by automating resume analysis and candidate ranking. Using NLP and Machine Learning algorithms, the system extracts relevant information from resumes and compares it with job descriptions to identify the most suitable candidates.

# 2. Literature Review
Several researchers have proposed AI-based recruitment systems to improve resume screening and candidate-job matching.

Here are short **summary-style descriptions** for each paper.

### 1. Roselinkiruba et al. (2025)

Roselinkiruba et al. proposed a Smart Recruiting System that uses **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Generative AI** to automate resume screening. The system performs resume parsing, candidate scoring, bias reduction, and provides a real-time recruiter dashboard, making the recruitment process faster, fairer, and more efficient. 

### 2. Raja Sekar et al. (2025)

Raja Sekar et al. developed a multimodal recruitment framework using **BERT, CNN, and Large Language Models (LLMs)**. The system automates resume parsing, job-role classification, and mock interviews while using fairness auditing to ensure accurate and ethical candidate selection. 

### 3. Mukil Ganesh et al. (2025)

Mukil Ganesh et al. introduced **ReSage AI**, an AI-powered resume screening framework that uses the **Google Gemini API**. It evaluates resume quality, provides intelligent content scoring, and offers personalized suggestions to help candidates improve their resumes and increase their chances of selection.

Skills

Certifications

5.3 Text Preprocessing

The extracted text is cleaned using:

Lowercase Conversion
Text Normalization

5.4 Job Description Processing

Recruiters upload job descriptions containing required skills, qualifications, and experience.

5.5 Feature Extraction

Relevant features are extracted using:

TF-IDF Vectorization
BERT Embeddings (Optional)
Skill Extraction
5.6 Candidate Matching

The system calculates similarity between resumes and job descriptions using:

Cosine Similarity
Semantic Matching
Skill Matching

5.7 Candidate Ranking

Candidates are ranked according to their matching scores.
